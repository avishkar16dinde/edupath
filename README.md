# 🎓 Career Path Simulator — From Class 10 to Career

### AI-powered education and career decision-support platform

**Career Path Simulator** is an AI-powered web platform designed to help students and parents make more informed education and career decisions after Class 10.

The platform creates a personalized student profile using:

- Academic performance
- Subjects and interests
- Aptitude and career preferences
- Budget
- Location preferences
- Financial-aid requirements
- Admission considerations

It then simulates and compares multiple education-to-career pathways instead of providing a single recommendation.

> **Explore • Compare • Simulate • Plan**

---

## 🚨 Problem

After Class 10, students face a large number of education and career choices.

A decision can involve:

- Which academic stream to choose
- Which degree or course to pursue
- Which institutions to consider
- Whether to study in India or abroad
- Tuition and living costs
- Admission requirements
- Scholarships and funding
- Education loans
- Alternative career routes
- What to do if the preferred pathway doesn't work out

Information about these decisions is often scattered across different sources.

Students and parents therefore have to manually collect information and compare options, making the decision-making process difficult and time-consuming.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

Career Path Simulator brings these factors into one decision-support platform.

Instead of simply telling a student:

> "You should choose Computer Science."

the platform presents multiple possible pathways and explains their trade-offs.
